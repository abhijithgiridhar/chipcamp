Chip Bot's Brain

💡 The big idea: two ways to send the same message

You⌨️ type 1 in the Serial Monitor➜📟 message 1➜⚙️ servo 1 moves
The AI📷 camera sees a blue card➜🧠 model says "blue"➜📟 message 1➜⚙️ servo 1 moves

Chip Bot does not know or care who sent the 1. A person or an AI, it moves servo 1 either way. Teachable Machine is the part that learns to turn what the camera sees into a message. Everything after that is the code you already wrote.

  1. 1Connect Chip Bot

    Upload the Chip Bot sketch from the Upload desk first. Close the Serial Monitor in the Arduino IDE, because only one program can use the USB cable at a time. You can try everything without Chip Bot, since the one on the right moves too.

  2. 2Choose how it sees

    Hold a coloured card, or a coloured page, in the middle of the camera picture. Chip Bot's brain looks at the middle and decides which colour it is. Nothing to train.

    Open Teachable Machine, choose Image Project, then Standard image model. Make 2 to 4 classes, for example Thumbs up, Peace sign and Nobody. Record about 40 pictures of each, then press Train Model.
